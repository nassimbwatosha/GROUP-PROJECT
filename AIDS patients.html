Increasing rate of HIV/AIDS patients

- The increasing rate of HIV/AIDS patients shows that people are getting infected everyday due to lack of proper knowledge about prevention, some don't get tested early and stigma also pevents people from seeking help.

- "Hope Beyond HIV/AIDS" addresses this problem through providing awareness and education on how HIV is transmitted and how it is prevented, forexample by the use of condoms during sexual intercourse to reduce new infections. The project also encourages regular testing and early diagnosis thus helping people to know their status early because early detection reduces the chances of spreading the virus. Furthermore, the project supports treatment and care through encouraging infected individuals to start and continue treatment thus reducing the viral load making transmission less likely. The project also fights stigma and discrimination through encouraging acceptance and support for people living with HIV thus making people more willing to seek treatment and advice.This project also reaches high-risk and undeserved groups through community outreach programs, thus providing information and support directly to the community.

- The increasing rate of HIV/AIDS patients shows that the disease is still spreading and affecting more lives, forexample globally, about 0.7% of adults are living with HIV, which is over 40 million people. And a higher percentage of 3.1% in Africa, with about 1.3 million new infections occuring every year. This shows that HIV is still spreading, which is why projects like "Hope Beyond HIV/AIDS" are needed to reduce new infections.
