Misunderstanding about HIV-free babies from HIV-positive mothers

Most people have different misconceptions about HIV positive mothers giving birth to either positive or negative babies. Some ignorant people think that if a pregnant mother is HIV positive , she gives birth to also a positive baby which is not true. If a pregnant lady is HIV positive , he or she should let the doctors know such that she is treated with special care in order for the baby not to contract HIV . Since HIV/AIDS is transmitted through liquids including blood. So pregnant mothers are advised to go for HIV testing and if they are alredy confirmed to be HIV positive, they should let the doctors know to save the newborn baby from contracting the virus.
