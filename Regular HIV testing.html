Regual HIV Testing

Why Regular HIV Testing is Important

Regular HIV testing helps people know their status early. When you know your status:

  • You can start treatment early if positive
  • You can protect your partner and others
  • You can live a long and healthy life

Early detection saves lives.

Who Should Test?

Everyone should consider testing, especially:

  • People who are sexually active
  • People with multiple partners
  • Pregnant women
  • Anyone who may have been exposed

How Often Should You Test?

It is recommended to test at least once a year. But if you are at higher risk, you should test every 3 to 6 months.

Breaking Stigma

We should not fear HIV testing. Testing is a sign of responsibility, not shame. Let us support each other and stop stigma.
